# About Us
Blood and Roit is an independent musical group from India founded by [sniffer](https://github.com/vasucp1207), [nogie](https://github.com/nogieman), [jordan](https://github.com/Aliqyan-21) and [wolve](https://github.com/shashankbhosagi). This organization is dedicated to musical experimental software tools we build for our music production and our passion.  We are non profit organization and only works for the causes that are align to our ideology and values. Our music is a voice for the common people our work is to amplify the unheard stories and social inequalities within our the society.
