# About Us
Blood and Riot is an independent musical group from India founded by [sniffer](https://github.com/vasucp1207), [nogie](https://github.com/nogieman), [jordan](https://github.com/Aliqyan-21) and [wolve](https://github.com/shashankbhosagi). This organization is dedicated to musical experimental software tools we build for our music production and our passion.  
We are non profit organization and only work for the causes that are align to our ideology and values.
Our music is a voice for the people and our work is to amplify unheard stories and social inequalities within our society.
We aim to become a front for the people and a way they express their resistance through our music.

Softwares in this organization is released under the [beerware license](license.txt).
